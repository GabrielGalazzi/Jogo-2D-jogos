## Sound effects

All files in Assets/Audio/SFX were synthesized for this project by `Tools/ArtGen/synth_sfx.py` (no samples, no third-party
material) and are released as CC0. Kenney.nl was checked first, but its downloads only work through the site's own
download page in a browser, which this project's rules excluded.
